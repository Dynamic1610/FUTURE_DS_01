05  /  Advisory

Insights & Recommendations

Four moves, ranked by expected impact on the bottom line.
FINDING 01

Furniture sells like a star and earns like an afterthought

Furniture brings in $742K — almost as much as Technology's $836K — but converts it into only a 2.5% margin, versus 17%+ for Office Supplies and Technology. Tables and Bookcases are outright unprofitable across the full order history.

Do thisRe-price or renegotiate cost on Tables and Bookcases before pushing more volume through them. More Furniture sales at current terms means more money lost, not more profit.
FINDING 02

Deep discounts are the biggest single driver of lost profit

Orders with 0–20% off are reliably profitable. The moment discounts cross ~20%, cumulative profit goes negative — and it gets worse from there. This pattern holds inside Furniture too: undiscounted Tables are profitable; discounted ones are the primary reason the sub-category loses money at all.

Do thisCap standard discounting at 20% except for cases with clear strategic reason (clearing dead stock, landing a large account), and require approval above that line.
FINDING 03

The West and East regions are the profit engine

West (14.9% margin) and East (13.5%) outperform Central (7.9%) by a wide margin, despite Central running a similar order volume to South. Central's underperformance tracks closely with heavier Furniture and discount exposure in that region.

Do thisInvestigate Central's discount approval habits and category mix specifically — it's the clearest region-level opportunity to recover margin without losing volume.
FINDING 04

Copiers, Labels, and Paper are the best-kept secret

Copiers (37% margin), Labels (44%), Envelopes (42%) and Paper (43%) are the highest-margin sub-categories in the business, but they're a small share of total sales. There's clear headroom to grow these lines without the margin risk that comes with Furniture.

Do thisBundle high-margin Office Supplies (Paper, Labels, Envelopes) into offers alongside Technology purchases, where basket sizes are already largest.
Source: Superstore order-level transaction data, · orders across customers · Prepared as an internship analytics deliverable.
